# Hi, I'm LaMeisha DuBose

Senior GRC professional building toward a future where compliance isn't a checklist. It's infrastructure.

## About

I'm a Senior GRC professional based in New York, building at the intersection of cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and AI governance. My background is in enterprise risk management, SOX ITGC, ISO 27001, HIPAA, and audit readiness — I've spent my career learning how governance programs actually work inside fast-moving technology organizations, and more importantly, where they break down.

What I'm working toward is the engineering side of that problem: translating policy into infrastructure, generating evidence artifacts automatically, and building continuous monitoring systems that catch drift in real time — not during the next annual audit. The goal isn't point-in-time compliance. It's compliance that runs.
