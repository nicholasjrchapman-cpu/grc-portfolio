    Reference templates¶

    Three fill-in-the-blank starting points for GRC documentation — a policy, a risk assessment, and a control mapping. Skip straight to whichever you need.

    Don't need these?

    These are optional. To remove them: delete docs/templates.md, then remove the Templates line from the nav: section in mkdocs.yml.

    • Policy template
    • Risk assessment template
    • Control mapping template

    Policy template¶

    Purpose¶

    What risk or requirement does this policy exist to address? One or two sentences.

    Scope¶

    Who and what systems does this policy apply to? Be specific — "all production AWS accounts," not "the cloud."

    Policy statement¶

    The actual rule. Write it the way it would be tested in an audit — clear enough that a control owner can't misread it.

    Roles & responsibilities¶

    Role Responsibility
    Policy Owner
    Control Owner
    Reviewer

    Enforcement¶

    How is this policy technically or procedurally enforced? Link to the automation or process, not just the intent.

    Exceptions¶

    Process for requesting and approving exceptions, and who signs off.

    Review cycle¶

    How often is this reviewed, and by whom. (e.g., "Annually, or upon material change to scope.")

    Related controls¶

    Map this to the frameworks it satisfies — SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST CSF, etc.

    Risk assessment template¶

    Asset description¶

    What is being assessed — the system, data flow, or process. Include what data it touches and where it lives.

    Threat scenarios¶

    # Threat Likelihood (1-5) Impact (1-5) Risk Score Notes
    1
    2
    3

    Scoring key: Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact. 1-6 = Low, 7-14 = Medium, 15-25 = High.

    Existing controls¶

    What's already in place that reduces likelihood or impact for each threat above?

    Residual risk¶

    After existing controls, what's left? This is the number that should drive the remediation plan.

    Remediation plan¶

    Action Owner Target Date Status

    Sign-off¶

    Who reviewed and accepted this assessment, and when.

    Control mapping template¶

    Maps one control across the frameworks you're tracking, so you can show — not just claim — cross-framework fluency.

    Control description¶

    What is this control actually doing? Written in plain language before you map it to any framework's jargon.

    Framework mapping¶

    Framework Control ID Control Language
    SOC 2
    ISO 27001
    NIST CSF
    PCI DSS

    Evidence type¶

    What counts as proof this control is operating — a screenshot, a config export, a log query result, an approval trail.

    Testing frequency¶

    How often is this control tested, and is that testing manual or automated?

    Gaps identified¶

    Where do the frameworks disagree, or where does your current evidence fall short of what an auditor would expect?
